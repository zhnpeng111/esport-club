Who We Are

The E-Sports Club is a student community that brings together gamers who enjoy teamwork, competition and learning new skills.

Our club organizes friendly matches, training sessions and activities for members throughout the year.

Vision

To become one of the leading university e-sports communities by promoting teamwork, innovation and excellence in competitive gaming.

Mission

  • Promote healthy gaming culture.
  • Develop teamwork and leadership.
  • Organize e-sports competitions.
  • Build a friendly gaming community.
